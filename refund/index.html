Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Plain language, no legalese.

What you are buying

The UGC NET Test Series is a digital product: ₹249, one-time, unlocks all 20 mock tests in one UGC NET subject of your choice. There is no subscription and nothing recurs. Tests 1–3 in every subject are free, so you can try the exact format before you pay. The price includes all applicable taxes; no extra charges apply.

Refunds

You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of purchase if you have not started any paid mock test (Tests 4–20) in that subject. Email officialchangeyourself@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with your payment ID (from your payment receipt) and the subject name. We will confirm within 2 working days and refund the full ₹249 to your original payment method within 5–7 working days.

If you have started a paid mock test (Test 4 or later) in that subject, the purchase is not refundable — the content has been used. The only exception is a technical fault on our side: if the tests would not load or your score was not recorded because of a platform error, tell us within 7 days and we will refund you in full even if you started a test.

When a refund is issued, access to that subject's paid tests (Tests 4–20) is removed from your account. Your free tests (1–3), your quiz history, and your other subjects are not affected.

Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice for the same subject, or charged for a payment that failed, email us with both payment IDs and we will refund the duplicate or failed charge in full. A failed payment never unlocks a subject, so there is nothing to lose access to.

Cancellation

There is nothing to cancel — the Test Series is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so no further payment will ever be taken. If you no longer want the tests, simply do not use them. If you want your account and all its data deleted, you can delete your account from your dashboard (Settings), or email us and we will delete it for you.

How to request a refund

Email officialchangeyourself@gmail.com with: (1) the email address on your ChangeYourself account, (2) the subject you bought, (3) your payment ID, and (4) the reason. We will reply within 2 working days.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at officialchangeyourself@gmail.com, or reach us on our YouTube channel, ChangeXed: youtube.com/@ChangeXed.
