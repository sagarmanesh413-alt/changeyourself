How this ranking works

Only full mock tests count, and only ones the server graded — a score can never be typed in or faked. Each person appears once, with their best percentage in the period (percentage, not raw marks, so a 100-question test is comparable with a 150-question one). Ties go to whoever reached the score first. The 10-question quiz rounds don't count — they aren't comparable to a full mock. Offline attempts count only after they sync and the server verifies them.

Rankings appear once at least 3 people have opted in and taken a mock test in that period — until then, the table stays empty rather than showing a misleading ranking. Your name, email and profile are never shown; the only public identity is the alias you choose (or a generated one like “M*** #4821”). You can change your alias or opt out at any time — opting out removes you from every ranking immediately.
