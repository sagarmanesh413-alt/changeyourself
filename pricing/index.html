What is UGC NET?

The University Grants Commission National Eligibility Test — the exam for Assistant Professor and Junior Research Fellowship in Indian universities. Our mocks cover Paper 1 and Paper 2 Psychology.

Each test has 150 questions (50 Paper 1 + 100 Paper 2 Psychology), a 3-hour timer, and a full review with the correct answer and an explanation for every question. Your best score is saved on your device.
