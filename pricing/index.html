Frequently Asked Questions

Are the mock tests free right now?

Mock Tests 1–3 are free for everyone. The remaining 17 tests in a subject unlock for ₹249 (one-time, per subject).

What is UGC NET?

The University Grants Commission National Eligibility Test — the exam for Assistant Professor and Junior Research Fellowship in Indian universities. Our mocks cover Paper 1 and Paper 2 in 10 subjects.

How do the mock tests work?

Each test has 150 questions (50 Paper 1 + 100 Paper 2 in your subject), a 3-hour timer, and a full review with the correct answer and an explanation for every question. Your scores are saved to your account when you're signed in (and on this device when you're not).

Can I take the tests on my phone?

Yes — the mock tests work fully in a mobile browser, no app install needed.
