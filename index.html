Questions, answered

Everything about the free tests, the ₹249 plan, offline mode and the leaderboard.

What do I get for ₹249?

All 20 mock tests in one UGC NET subject of your choice, as a one-time payment. That includes offline downloads for eligible tests, the full exam experience, performance tracking across all 20 tests, leaderboard participation and achievements. You pay per subject — only for the subjects you want.

How many mock tests are included?

20 per subject. Each test has 150 questions (50 Paper 1 + 100 Paper 2), a 3-hour timer, and a score out of 300 with a full review and an explanation for every question.

Which mock tests are free?

Tests 1–3 in every subject, plus the quick quiz — 100 most-probable questions in 10-question rounds.

Can I take downloaded tests without internet?

Yes. Download a test while you're online, then take it with no connection. Your answers save on your device and sync when you reconnect.

How long do offline downloads remain available?

30 days from download. After that, download the test again while you're online.

When are offline scores verified?

Only after your attempt syncs and the server grades it. Until then your result shows as pending — never as a verified score. If the package is outdated or your entitlement was removed, the attempt can't be graded, but it stays visible on your device.

How does the anonymous leaderboard work?

Your best verified mock-test percentage in the chosen period, ranked against everyone who opted in. Ties go to whoever reached the score first. Quiz rounds and unverified offline attempts don't count.

Will my name or email appear on the leaderboard?

Never. Only the public alias you choose — or a generated one like “M*** #4821” — is shown.

Can I opt out of the leaderboard?

Yes, any time. Opting out removes you from every ranking immediately. Your alias is kept, so you can opt back in later.

Can I track my previous mock-test performance?

Yes — your dashboard keeps your full mock-test history, scores, personal bests, subject progress, weekly summaries and recommendations. Sign in to see it.

What if I change my mind after paying?

Full refund within 7 days if you haven't started a paid test in that subject. If you started one, no refund except for a platform technical fault. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
