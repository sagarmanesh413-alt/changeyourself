Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Plain language, no legalese.

What this site is

ChangeYourself is a website with optional user accounts. You can use the mock tests and the quiz without signing in and without giving us anything about yourself. If you sign in (with Google or an email code), we store your account and your test history so you can see them on your dashboard.

What is stored — and where

Two kinds of storage:

  • In your own browser, on your own device, and nowhere else: your theme choice (dark or light), and your best scores on the UGC NET mock tests and the quick quiz when you are signed out.
  • In your account, on our database (Supabase): your email; your name if you provide it — Google shares your name and profile photo when you sign in with Google; the profile details you fill in on your dashboard (date of birth, qualification, work status, phone, city, target subject); every mock test and quiz round you complete while signed in (subject, set, score, date); and your plan and any purchases.

Other users cannot see your account data. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes only the browser-only items above.

Payments

We do not take payments on this site today. The paid plans (Pro, Premium, Institutional, and the ₹199 Test Series plan) are not available to buy yet, and no payment details are collected. When payments are added, they will be handled by the payment provider, and this policy will be updated to say so.

Third parties

Signing in runs on Supabase — when you sign in, your browser talks to Supabase's servers, and the sign-in code loads from a content delivery network. If you sign in with Google, Google's own privacy policy applies to Google. This site loads no analytics scripts and no tracking scripts. It links to our YouTube channel, ChangeXed — if you follow that link, YouTube's own privacy policy applies there.

Your data

If you have an account, you can see and edit your details on your dashboard, and you can sign out at any time. To delete your account and everything stored with it, reach us on our YouTube channel, ChangeXed — there is no self-serve delete button yet. Your best scores and theme choice when signed out live only in your browser; clearing your browser data deletes them.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us on our YouTube channel, ChangeXed: youtube.com/@ChangeXed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
