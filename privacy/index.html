Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Plain language, no legalese.

What this site is

ChangeYourself is a static website. There are no user accounts, no login, and no server that stores your information. You can use the mock tests and the quiz without giving us anything about yourself.

What is stored — and where

Two things are saved in your own browser, on your own device, and nowhere else:

  • Your theme choice (dark or light).
  • Your best scores on the UGC NET mock tests and the quick quiz.

Nothing about you is sent to us or to any other server. Clearing your browser data removes both of these.

Payments

We do not take payments on this site today. The paid plans (Pro, Premium, Institutional, and the ₹199 Test Series plan) are not available to buy yet, and no payment details are collected. When payments are added, they will be handled by the payment provider, and this policy will be updated to say so.

Third parties

This site loads no analytics scripts and no tracking scripts. It links to our YouTube channel, ChangeXed — if you follow that link, YouTube's own privacy policy applies there.

Your data

Because there are no accounts, there is nothing for us to delete. Your best scores and theme choice live only in your browser; clearing your browser data deletes them.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us on our YouTube channel, ChangeXed: youtube.com/@ChangeXed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
