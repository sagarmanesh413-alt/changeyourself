The service

ChangeYourself provides free UGC NET mock tests (all 20 in each subject) and a free quick quiz, plus information pages about career development and learning. Some features described on the site — AI Assistant, resume builder, mentorship — are not built yet and are marked "Coming soon" wherever they appear.

Free

The Test Series is free. No payment is taken on this site, and no subscription is created.

Using the mock tests and quiz

The mock tests and the quiz are for your own exam preparation. Please do not copy, resell, or republish the questions. The mock tests need a free account — sign in first, and your scores are saved to your account. The quick quiz can be taken without signing in; then your best scores are saved only in your browser, on your device.

No guarantees

We work hard to keep the questions and explanations accurate, but we do not guarantee any exam result. Use the tests as practice, alongside your own study.

Accounts and your data

You can create an account by signing in with Google or an email code. Your profile and test history are saved to your account and shown on your dashboard. Keep your sign-in details to yourself. To delete your account and its data, contact us on our YouTube channel, ChangeXed.
